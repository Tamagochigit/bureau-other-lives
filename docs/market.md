# Market

No competitor research, market size or demand evidence was requested or collected. “Bureau of other lives” is a working concept name, not a trademark clearance or an originality claim.

Treat the prototype as an opportunity to observe actual use. One completed activity and a later voluntary return are more informative than an invented demand percentage. Commercial positioning should follow that evidence.
