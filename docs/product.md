# Product

A personal Russian web application combines small adventures, traditions and a compass grounded in the user's own ratings. The outcome is one real attempt followed by an honest reflection, not an inferred personality assessment.

Current owner direction, 2026-10-05: home starts with «Удиви меня», with no time/place form. It draws from the whole catalog while avoiding recently completed experiences when alternatives exist. Existing active progress remains visible below; viewing another mission never replaces it. Beginning a different mission still requires explicit confirmation. Catalog filters remain optional inside their disclosure.

Acceptance: open a surprise, inspect three steps, begin/resume, complete an adapted or partial experience, record a 1–5 rating and optional note/repeat preference, reopen/edit the diary, inspect honest category averages, save favourites/traditions, export and safely merge a backup.

Friends use an existing messenger. Add/edit/remove a local name and Telegram username; «Написать» opens Telegram. Proposing a mission prepares only its public catalog title/link, either in that contact's draft or Telegram's chat chooser. The user sends the message in Telegram. Opening the app, a mission or friends does not contact Telegram or send anything. Contacts are local shortcuts, not verified identities, mutual friendships or a synchronised Telegram address book.

This is a standalone GitHub/Pages product. No product login, platform identity, chat backend, message archive, bot, payments, billable AI, cloud diary sync or unread/online claims. Conversations and existing accounts belong to Telegram. Embedding a full messaging client is a separate decision requiring a verified production provider/account flow.

Diary v1 and backups remain compatible; contacts have a separate local v1 key and are not in diary exports. Historical custom-chat decisions/receipts in [changes](changes.md) and [decisions](decisions.md) describe earlier releases only. [Requirements](requirements.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and [testing](testing.md) define current acceptance and limits. Review when product scope, start or messenger behaviour changes.

2026-10-05 Android milestone: a direct-install pilot shares the authored catalog and diary semantics, bundles content offline and delegates Telegram externally. It has a warm open-door icon and a count of distinct completed roles in the diary. No payment or retention result is claimed. Audience defaults to adults seeking novelty because the optional question had no answer. [Android](android.md) owns runtime/recovery; [motivation and monetisation](monetization.md) owns commercial hypotheses.
